# Community expansion
Owner-only web editing. Autoresponders reuse chatRules and existing cooldowns. Role panels one voluntary role each, buttons/menu or ✅/❌ reaction add actions; removal of a reaction does not revoke roles. Reactions require appropriate bot permissions to remove them for repeat use. Existing roleButtons registry keeps staff and rewards safeguards. Retiring a panel stops future grants but does not remove already assigned roles. Starboard only posts a link to a public source channel; no content or attachments copied, at most 500 remembered posts per guild. Booster Center exposes the existing /rc and Boosts rewards, not a competing system. New settings use Store; no Supabase adapter changes. Validate Discord events and permissions before production.
